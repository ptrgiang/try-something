# Code of Conduct

Try Something adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct, for the repository and for the site's community features.

In short: be kind and constructive, assume good intent, and don't harass, demean, dox or spam anyone. Critique work, not people.

## Reporting

Report conduct issues privately to the maintainers by opening a confidential report through the repository's **Security → Report a vulnerability** form (choose the title prefix `[conduct]`), or by contacting a maintainer directly. Reports are handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, submissions, commits or other contributions, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
